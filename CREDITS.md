## Audio

All 102 sounds in `public/assets/audio/` were made for this project and are released as CC0. Nothing was sampled from third-party recordings. The generator scripts are in `tools/audio/` (see its README). They use offline DSP synthesis: modal and stick-slip physical models, filtered and granular noise, formant synthesis, and synthetic convolution reverbs.

- **Mother's voice, whispers, radio voice:** rendered with [Piper TTS](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (MIT) using the `en_US-ljspeech-high` voice by Bryce Beattie ([model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high/MODEL_CARD)). It was trained on the [LJ Speech dataset](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/), which is **public domain**. The output was then heavily processed: pitch and formant shifts, doubling, sub-octave undertones, a channel-vocoder whisper, reversal, telephone band-limiting and reverb.
- **Pitch shifting and time stretching:** [Rubber Band](https://breakfastquay.com/rubberband/), via ffmpeg, as a build-time tool only.
- **The music-box lullaby and the humming:** an original melody written for VIGIL.
